## Numbered Findings List

### Finding #1 — [HIGH] R-02 (Copywriting: Prohibited Em Dash `—` in UI Prose)
- **Rule:** R-02 (Copywriting — Hard Gate)
- **Location:**
  - `src/components/HeroSection.tsx` (lines 52–53)
  - `src/components/HowIBuild.tsx` (line 29)
  - `src/components/EnhancedTechStack.tsx` (lines 30, 77)
  - `src/components/MetricsSection.tsx` (line 11)
  - `src/components/ProjectCard.tsx` (line 41)
  - `src/components/ContactForm.tsx` (lines 46, 79)
  - `src/app/page.tsx` (line 55)
  - `src/app/work/page.tsx` (line 64)
  - `src/app/about/page.tsx` (lines 12, 15, 46)
  - `src/resources/content.js` (lines 23, 60, 71, 90, 131, 140, 211, 238, 271, 293, 302, 394, 401, 408, 414)
- **Reason:** R-02 strictly forbids em dashes (`—`) in UI copy, titles, and text because it is one of the most recognizable AI writing artifacts.
- **Proposed Fix:** Replace all instances of `—` with commas (`,`), colons (`:`), hyphens (`-`), or parentheses `()` while preserving natural, human phrasing.

### Finding #2 — [MEDIUM] R-06 & User Directive (Unified Font Family Across the Entire Site)
- **Rule:** R-06 (Typography — Purpose-Gate) & User Directive (*"Sesuaikan font agar seragam ke satu font family di seluruh situs"*)
- **Location:**
  - `src/resources/once-ui.config.js` (lines 29–62)
  - `src/resources/custom.css` (lines 1–50, font tokens and variables)
  - `src/app/layout.tsx` (lines 57–63)
  - `src/components/Header.module.scss` (lines 44, 174, 255)
  - `src/components/mdx.tsx` (lines 117, creating headings)
- **Reason:** Currently, the site splits typography across three families (`Newsreader` serif for headings, `Source Sans 3` for body/labels, and `IBM Plex Mono` for code). The user requested unifying the font family so the entire site has a cohesive, single typeface identity.
- **Proposed Fix:**
  - Standardize headings, display titles, body, and navigation on one unified sans-serif family (`Source Sans 3` or `Inter`), or standardizing the primary type system while keeping monospace exclusively for syntax/terminal code blocks.
  - Align font-weights (`400` body, `500`/`600` headings, `700` titles), fluid clamp scales, and line-heights across `custom.css` and all component SCSS modules.

### Finding #3 — [LOW] R-08 (Button Decoration Arrows)
- **Rule:** R-08 (Button Arrows — Purpose-Gate)
- **Location:**
  - `src/components/HeroSection.tsx` (lines 104, 108: `↓`, `→`)
  - `src/components/CTASection.tsx` (lines 105, 114: `→`, `↗`)
- **Reason:** R-08 advises that arrows (`→`, `↗`) should not be the automatic default ornament on every CTA button unless they serve a distinct directional or external navigation purpose.
- **Proposed Fix:** Keep the external link indicator on external tabs (`↗` for scheduling/docs) and in-page down-arrow (`↓` for jumping to work), but remove redundant decorative arrows from standard buttons or write their specific purpose into documentation.

### Finding #4 — [LOW] R-03 / R-32 (Mobile Tap Target Dimensions)
- **Rule:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) & R-32 (Accessibility)
- **Location:**
  - `src/components/Header.module.scss` (`.mobileToggle`: 40×40px)
  - `src/components/ThemeToggle.tsx` (38×38px button)
- **Reason:** WCAG 2.5.5 and Apple/Android touch guidelines recommend a minimum 44×44px hit target area for mobile tap targets to prevent missed taps.
- **Proposed Fix:** Increase the hit target area to 44×44px (using transparent padding or `min-width: 44px; min-height: 44px;`) while maintaining the sleek visual border.

---

### Finding #5 — [LOW] R-31 & R-37 (Declaration of Antislop Dials & Design Intent)
- **Rule:** R-31 (Every Decision Must Have a Reason) & R-37 (Design Direction Dials)
- **Location:**
  - `src/resources/custom.css` (header comment)
- **Reason:** Antislop requires explicit declaration of design dials to prevent falling into neutral sterile defaults or unguided trend-stacking.
- **Proposed Fix:** Declare and adhere to the following project dials:
  - **ENERGY 1 (Calm)**: Production engineering clarity, understated tones, content-first.
  - **RHYTHM 2 (Balanced)**: Structured flow with intentional editorial breaks between selected case studies and tech stacks.
  - **MOTION 2 (Smooth Transitions)**: Refined micro-interactions, subtle scroll triggers, no excessive floating or bounce.
